Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how personal data is processed in the Piece by Word mobile game (the “Game”), offered by Serra Sadır under the Zorse Studio brand. Users in Türkiye can find further detail in the KVKK Privacy Notice.

Controller

Serra Sadır (Zorse Studio brand). Address: Şenlikköy Mah. Orman Sk. No:5/3, 34153 Bakırköy/Istanbul, Türkiye. Email: info@zorsestudio.com.

Summary

The Game has no server of its own and no account system. We do not ask for your name, email or card details. Your progress stays on your device. We use Google and Apple services for ads, leaderboards and purchases.

Stored on your device

Level progress, unfinished level saves, coin and hint balances, purchase delivery records, weekly challenge attempts and sound and vibration settings are kept only on your device and are not sent to us. If you delete the Game this data is deleted, including purchased coins and hints.

Ads and tracking choices

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch them. Depending on your region you may see an ad consent form, and on iOS you may be asked for tracking permission. If you decline, non-personalized ads are shown and the Game works the same. You can change your choice in the in-game Settings, where offered, or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe your child has shared data, contact us.

Retention and deletion

We hold no account that can be matched to you. Data on your device is deleted when you delete the Game. For Game Center scores and advertising data, use the settings of the relevant company or write to us for help.

International users

EU, EEA and UK: We process data on the basis of contract to provide leaderboards and purchases, legitimate interests for non-personalized ads and security, and your consent for personalized ads. You can withdraw consent at any time and complain to your local data protection authority.

United States: We do not sell personal data for money. Personalized advertising through Google may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some state laws; you can opt out using the ad choices above.

Transfers: Our service providers may process data outside your country, particularly in the United States.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to processing and to complain. Send requests to info@zorsestudio.com. As there are no accounts, we may be unable to identify you within the Game; in that case we will direct you to the relevant provider.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page.
