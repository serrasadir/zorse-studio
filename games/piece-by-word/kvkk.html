Support correspondence is kept for at most 2 years after a request is resolved. Data held by Apple and Google is subject to their retention periods. When the period ends, data is deleted, destroyed or anonymized.

Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, to learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used accordingly, to know third parties to whom it is transferred, to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, to request deletion or destruction where conditions are met and notification of these to recipients, to object to an adverse result arising from automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Applications

Send requests to info@zorsestudio.com. Applications are concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest; if the process involves an additional cost, the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board may apply. If you are not satisfied with the result you may complain to the Board.
