Support

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Write to us with questions, bug reports and data requests: info@zorsestudio.com. For faster help include your device model and iOS version, and your store receipt for purchase issues.

FAQ

I did not receive my purchase. Close and reopen the Game and check your internet connection. If the problem continues, write to us with your receipt.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by the store. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the Game? Yes. Scores, coins and skins are stored only on your device.

I do not appear on the leaderboard. Check that you are signed in under iOS Settings > Game Center.

How do I change my nickname? In iOS Settings > Game Center.

How do I change my ad preferences? Where offered, in the Game under Settings > Privacy Choices, or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
